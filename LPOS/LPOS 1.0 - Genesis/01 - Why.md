Chapter 1 — Why

“Every great journey begins with a testimony.”

There are moments in life that quietly change everything.

For me, that moment did not begin with an idea for an application, a business, or even a ministry.

It began with the book of Proverbs.

I have always loved reading Proverbs. Its wisdom continually spoke to my heart, but over time I discovered something that changed the way I prayed forever.

Instead of simply reading a verse and moving on, I stopped.

I began responding to what I had just read.

If Scripture spoke about wisdom, I prayed for wisdom.

If it spoke about humility, I asked God to humble my heart.

If it warned against temptation, I prayed that God would guard my eyes, my thoughts, and my decisions.

God’s Word was no longer only something I read.

It became a conversation.

Around 2020, during the COVID season, life slowed down. It gave me more time to read, reflect, and pray. During that season my friend, Matt Roussin, was also reading through Proverbs, and that encouraged me even more to stay faithful to God’s Word.

Something remarkable began happening.

The same verses I had read before suddenly became personal.

Month after month, year after year, I continued reading Proverbs. Although the words on the page never changed, I did.

Every month I found myself discovering something new in verses I thought I already knew.

God was patiently shaping my heart through the very same Scriptures.

As I continued praying through Proverbs, I noticed something even more important.

My heart was changing.

The situations that once tempted me reminded me of the very prayers I had been praying.

Verses I had spoken to God in the morning returned to my mind throughout the day.

The Holy Spirit used God’s Word to guard my heart, my thoughts, and my actions.

I wasn’t simply learning Proverbs.

I was allowing God to transform me through it.

That experience changed my understanding of prayer.

Prayer was no longer something I occasionally did.

It became my response to God’s Word.

Sometimes it was praise.

Sometimes gratitude.

Sometimes repentance.

Sometimes asking for wisdom.

Sometimes quietly asking for God’s help.

But every prayer began with something God had already spoken.

I know God is real.

I know He hears our prayers.

I know His Word is living and active.

I have experienced His faithfulness, and I have watched Him use Scripture to transform my own life.

That is why LetUsPray exists.

Not because I wanted to build another Bible application.

Not because I wanted to create another Christian resource.

But because I wanted to share the experience that God had graciously given me.

I began wondering…

What if every Bible verse naturally led someone into prayer?

What if reading Scripture became the beginning of a conversation with God instead of the end of a reading plan?

What if people who didn’t know what to pray could simply respond to the very words God had already given them?

That simple question became the beginning of LetUsPray.

LetUsPray is not the destination.

It is simply a tool.

A gentle invitation to slow down.

To open God’s Word.

To listen.

And then to respond.

It does not matter whether it is someone’s first prayer or their thousandth.

What matters is that they chose to pray.

If LetUsPray helps even one person open Scripture, speak honestly with God, and begin experiencing the transforming work of the Holy Spirit, then it has fulfilled its purpose.

Everything else belongs to God.

Because Scripture informs the mind.

Prayer opens the heart.

God transforms the person.
