Chapter 2 — The Calling

“A calling is something we receive, not something we invent.”

More Than an Application

LetUsPray was never intended to become another Bible application.

It was never created to compete with existing Christian resources or to replace the local church, personal Bible study, or a believer’s own relationship with God.

LetUsPray exists for one simple purpose:

To help people pray through Scripture, one Bible verse at a time.

That purpose guides every decision we make.

Every feature.

Every prayer.

Every animation.

Every line of code.

If a decision does not deepen prayer or help someone engage with God’s Word, it does not belong in LetUsPray.

⸻

A Calling, Not an Idea

There are many good ideas in the world.

Some become businesses.

Some become products.

Some become ministries.

LetUsPray began differently.

It was born out of a personal experience with God through His Word.

Reading Scripture became praying Scripture.

Praying Scripture became transformation.

Transformation created a desire to share that experience with others.

That desire became LetUsPray.

We do not believe this project belongs to us.

We believe we have been entrusted with it.

Because of that, we choose to approach every decision with humility, wisdom, and prayer.

⸻

Our Responsibility

Every person who opens LetUsPray is more than a user.

They are a person created in the image of God.

Some will open the app with joy.

Some will open it in grief.

Some will be searching for hope.

Others may not know how to pray at all.

Every prayer we write should respect that reality.

We never want to overwhelm people.

We never want to manipulate emotions.

We simply want to place Scripture before them and gently invite them into conversation with God.

⸻

What Success Means

The world often measures success through downloads, ratings, revenue, or popularity.

Those things may come, or they may not.

They are not our definition of success.

For LetUsPray, success is measured differently.

Success is seeing someone develop a deeper love for God’s Word.

Success is helping someone build a consistent prayer life.

Success is seeing marriages strengthened, families praying together, young people discovering their identity in Christ, and believers growing in spiritual maturity.

Success is reading testimonies of lives changed because people responded to God’s Word in prayer.

Every testimony belongs to God.

⸻

Our Calling

Our calling is not to build the biggest Christian application.

Our calling is not to create the most impressive technology.

Our calling is to faithfully build the prayer experience God has entrusted to us.

We believe Scripture is living and active.

We believe prayer changes hearts.

We believe God transforms lives.

Everything we build exists to support that truth.

If LetUsPray helps even one person stop, open the Bible, and sincerely speak with God, then we rejoice.

Not because we built something successful.

But because someone drew closer to Jesus.

That is our calling.

And by God’s grace, we pray we remain faithful to it for generations to come.
