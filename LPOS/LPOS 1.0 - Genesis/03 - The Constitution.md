Chapter 3 — The Constitution

“Principles protect the mission when circumstances change.”

Our Foundation

Technology will change.

Design trends will change.

Artificial intelligence will change.

Platforms, devices, and the way people interact with software will continue to evolve.

The mission of LetUsPray must not.

This Constitution exists to preserve the original heart of LetUsPray and to guide every future decision. These principles are not suggestions. They are the foundation upon which the project is built.

Whenever uncertainty arises, we return here.

⸻

Principle 1 — Scripture Comes First

Everything begins with God’s Word.

Scripture is the foundation of every prayer, every journey, and every decision within LetUsPray.

We do not build around opinions.

We build around Scripture.

⸻

Principle 2 — Prayer Is Our Response

Reading Scripture is the beginning.

Prayer is the response.

LetUsPray exists to help people respond to God’s Word by entering into a sincere conversation with Him.

⸻

Principle 3 — God Brings Transformation

Scripture informs the mind.

Prayer opens the heart.

God transforms the person.

Transformation is never produced by technology.

It is the work of the Holy Spirit.

LetUsPray simply creates space for people to encounter God’s Word and respond in prayer.

⸻

Principle 4 — Jesus Remains the Focus

The purpose of LetUsPray is not to point people toward an application.

It is to point people toward Jesus Christ.

If the application ever becomes more important than the One it exists to serve, we have lost our way.

⸻

Principle 5 — Genesis Remains Genesis

Genesis captures the original heart of LetUsPray.

It is preserved as a historical document.

Future versions may expand upon Genesis, but they must never rewrite it.

Every future decision should honor the foundation established here.

Genesis remains Genesis.

⸻

Principle 6 — Never Rush Genesis

Strong foundations require patience.

We choose thoughtful decisions over hurried ones.

We choose excellence over convenience.

We choose faithfulness over speed.

Every meaningful decision deserves careful consideration.

⸻

Principle 7 — People Before Features

Every feature represents a real person.

Every screen may be opened by someone carrying fear, grief, joy, hope, or uncertainty.

Before asking,

“What can this feature do?”

we first ask,

“How will this help someone pray?”

⸻

Principle 8 — Simplicity Reveals Beauty

The purpose of design is not to impress.

The purpose of design is to remove distractions.

Beauty should quietly support prayer, never compete with it.

Every interaction should help people focus on God rather than the application itself.

⸻

Principle 9 — Truth Before Popularity

We will not chase trends simply because they are popular.

Every decision must be measured against Scripture and the mission of LetUsPray.

Popularity may come.

Faithfulness must remain.

⸻

Principle 10 — AI Serves the Mission

Artificial intelligence is a tool.

It assists.

It organizes.

It accelerates.

It never replaces discernment, wisdom, Scripture, or prayer.

Humans remain responsible for the mission.

AI assists in carrying it forward.

⸻

Principle 11 — Excellence Is an Act of Stewardship

Everything entrusted to us deserves our best effort.

Every prayer.

Every animation.

Every line of code.

Every illustration.

Every interaction.

We pursue excellence not to impress others, but because we desire to faithfully steward what God has entrusted to us.

⸻

Principle 12 — To God Be the Glory Alone

Every testimony belongs to God.

Every changed life belongs to God.

Every answered prayer belongs to God.

Every success belongs to God.

LetUsPray exists to faithfully serve.

God alone receives the glory.

⸻

The Constitution

Whenever difficult decisions arise…

Return to Scripture.

Return to prayer.

Return to Genesis.

If a decision honors these principles, move forward with confidence.

If it does not, have the courage to say no.

Principles protect the mission.

That is why this Constitution exists.
