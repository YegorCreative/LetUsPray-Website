Chapter 4 — The Promise

“Promises create trust.”

Our Promise

Every application makes promises.

Some promise convenience.

Some promise entertainment.

Some promise productivity.

LetUsPray makes different promises.

These promises shape every decision we make and every experience we create.

They are commitments we intend to keep.

⸻

We Promise to Honor Scripture

Scripture will always remain the foundation of LetUsPray.

We will never place personal opinion above God’s Word.

Every prayer, devotional, journey, and feature will begin with Scripture.

When Scripture speaks, we listen.

⸻

We Promise to Encourage Prayer

Prayer is not an obligation.

Prayer is an invitation.

Our purpose is not to tell people what to think.

Our purpose is to encourage sincere conversations with God.

Every journey should help someone grow in confidence, consistency, and intimacy with Him.

⸻

We Promise to Build with Excellence

God deserves our best.

Every detail matters.

The words we write.

The animations we create.

The accessibility we provide.

The performance we deliver.

The beauty we design.

Excellence is one way we steward what has been entrusted to us.

⸻

We Promise to Remain Accessible

Prayer should never become a luxury.

God’s Word was freely given.

Our desire is to make LetUsPray available to anyone seeking to grow closer to God.

Accessibility is not simply a technical feature.

It reflects our heart.

⸻

We Promise to Listen

We do not assume we have every answer.

We welcome thoughtful feedback.

We value testimonies.

We learn from pastors, teachers, families, young believers, mature believers, and those who are still searching.

Listening is one way we remain teachable.

⸻

We Promise to Protect Trust

If someone journals within LetUsPray…

Those thoughts deserve care.

If someone records a testimony…

That story deserves respect.

If someone entrusts us with personal information…

We will treat that responsibility with integrity.

Trust is earned slowly and lost quickly.

We choose to earn it every day.

⸻

We Promise to Keep Jesus at the Center

Every feature should quietly point beyond itself.

Our goal is not to create dependence upon LetUsPray.

Our goal is to encourage dependence upon Christ.

If people remember the application more than the Savior, we have failed our purpose.

⸻

Our Promise to Every User

Whether you have followed Jesus for decades…

Whether you are opening a Bible for the very first time…

Whether you are joyful…

Broken…

Searching…

Or simply curious…

You are welcome here.

May this application help you slow down.

Open God’s Word.

Pray honestly.

And discover the faithfulness of God for yourself.

That is our promise.
