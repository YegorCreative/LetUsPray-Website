Chapter 9 — The Stewardship

“Everything entrusted to us deserves faithful stewardship.”

We Are Stewards

Nothing about LetUsPray truly belongs to us.

Not the vision.

Not the code.

Not the prayers.

Not the testimonies.

Everything has been entrusted to our care for a season.

Our responsibility is to steward it faithfully.

⸻

Stewarding God’s Word

Scripture is not content.

It is God’s living Word.

Every verse deserves reverence.

Every prayer deserves humility.

Every devotional deserves careful thought.

We approach God’s Word with gratitude and respect, never treating it as material to simply fill an application.

⸻

Stewarding People

Behind every download is a person.

Behind every testimony is a story.

Behind every prayer request is someone carrying hope, grief, fear, joy, or uncertainty.

People are never statistics.

They are the reason LetUsPray exists.

We choose compassion over convenience.

Relationship over numbers.

Faithfulness over growth.

⸻

Stewarding Trust

Trust is one of the greatest gifts people can give us.

If someone writes a private journal…

Shares a testimony…

Saves personal prayers…

Or simply spends time within LetUsPray…

We will treat that trust with integrity.

Privacy is stewardship.

Security is stewardship.

Transparency is stewardship.

⸻

Stewarding Resources

Time.

Money.

Technology.

Talent.

Every resource has been entrusted to us.

We choose simplicity over waste.

Purpose over excess.

Quality over shortcuts.

Generosity over selfish ambition.

We recognize that every resource ultimately belongs to God.

⸻

Stewarding Influence

If LetUsPray grows, influence will grow with it.

Influence is never a reward.

It is a responsibility.

The greater the influence, the greater the humility required.

Every platform should point beyond itself.

Every success should lead to gratitude.

Every testimony should glorify God.

⸻

Stewarding the Future

One day others will continue this work.

Developers.

Designers.

Pastors.

Writers.

Volunteers.

Leaders.

We desire to leave them something healthy.

Something understandable.

Something faithful.

Something worthy of continuing.

That is why Genesis exists.

⸻

Faithful Stewards

When our work is finished, we hope one thing can be said.

Not that we built something impressive.

Not that we built something popular.

But that we faithfully stewarded what God entrusted to us.

That is enough.
