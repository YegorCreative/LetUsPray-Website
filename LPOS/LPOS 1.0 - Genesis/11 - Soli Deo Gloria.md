Epilogue — Soli Deo Gloria

“To God be the glory alone.”

Every page of Genesis began with a simple desire:

To help people pray through Scripture, one Bible verse at a time.

If these pages ever inspire someone…

May they be inspired to seek God.

If these principles help build something meaningful…

May every meaningful result point back to Him.

If LetUsPray helps people fall in love with God’s Word…

Praise belongs to God.

If marriages are restored…

Praise belongs to God.

If families begin praying together…

Praise belongs to God.

If someone discovers hope in the middle of despair…

Praise belongs to God.

If one young person discovers their identity in Christ…

Praise belongs to God.

If one believer returns to prayer after years away…

Praise belongs to God.

If one person opens the Bible for the first time because of LetUsPray…

Praise belongs to God.

Nothing in these pages was written to make much of ourselves.

We are builders.

We are stewards.

We are servants.

Christ alone is the foundation.

Scripture remains our authority.

Prayer remains our response.

The Holy Spirit remains the One who transforms hearts.

Everything we build will one day pass away.

The Kingdom of God will not.

May LetUsPray always remain a faithful tool.

Never the destination.

Always an invitation.

An invitation to open God’s Word.

To speak honestly with God.

To trust Him more deeply.

And to walk with Jesus one day at a time.

If one day LetUsPray becomes larger than we ever imagined…

May we remain humble.

If one day it reaches nations we never visit…

May we remain grateful.

If one day our names are forgotten…

May the name of Jesus continue to be remembered.

That is enough.

That has always been enough.

Our Closing Prayer

Lord,

Everything we have belongs to You.

Every idea.

Every prayer.

Every line of code.

Every conversation.

Every testimony.

Every person who will one day open LetUsPray.

Guard this mission.

Keep us humble.

Keep us faithful.

Protect us from pride.

Protect us from distraction.

Protect us from building something that points to ourselves.

May Your Word always remain first.
