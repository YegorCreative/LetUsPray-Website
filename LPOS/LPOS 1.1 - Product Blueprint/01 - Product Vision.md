LPOS 1.1 — Product Blueprint

Chapter 1 — Product Vision

“Build a prayer experience that helps people encounter God through His Word.”

Our Vision

LetUsPray exists to help people pray through Scripture, one Bible verse at a time.

It is more than a reading application.

More than a prayer journal.

More than a devotional.

It is an invitation.

An invitation to slow down, open God’s Word, and respond to it through prayer.

Everything we build should make that invitation clearer.

⸻

The Problem We Are Solving

Many Christians desire a deeper prayer life.

Many faithfully read the Bible but are unsure how to respond to what they have just read.

Others know they should pray but simply do not know where to begin.

Some feel overwhelmed.

Some feel distracted.

Some have lost consistency.

Some are opening the Bible for the very first time.

LetUsPray exists to bridge that moment between reading and responding.

When God speaks through Scripture, we help people answer with prayer.

⸻

Our Product Philosophy

Reading Scripture changes what we know.

Praying Scripture changes how we live.

The goal of LetUsPray is not merely to increase biblical knowledge.

The goal is to help people allow God’s Word to shape their hearts through prayer.

Every Bible verse becomes an opportunity to respond.

Every prayer becomes an opportunity to grow.

Every day becomes another opportunity to walk with Christ.

⸻

What Makes LetUsPray Different

Many applications help people read Scripture.

Many applications help people track habits.

Many applications provide devotionals.

LetUsPray combines something deeply simple:

Read one verse.

Pray that verse.

Reflect.

Continue the conversation with God.

This rhythm becomes the heartbeat of the entire application.

⸻

Our Experience

We want people to leave LetUsPray feeling closer to God than when they opened it.

Not because the application entertained them.

Not because it impressed them.

But because it helped remove distractions and guided them toward a sincere conversation with God.

Peace should replace hurry.

Reflection should replace distraction.

Prayer should replace uncertainty.

Scripture should become personal.

⸻

Who We Serve

LetUsPray is for lifelong believers.

It is for people who have never opened a Bible.

It is for children learning to pray.

It is for parents praying with their families.

It is for students.

Pastors.

Missionaries.

Grandparents.

People celebrating.

People grieving.

People searching.

People doubting.

People hoping.

Everyone is invited.

⸻

Our Definition of Success

Success is not measured by downloads.

Success is not measured by revenue.

Success is not measured by popularity.

Success is measured by transformed lives.

A person returning to prayer.

A family praying together.

A young person discovering their identity in Christ.

A marriage strengthened.

A testimony written.

A believer falling in love with God’s Word again.

If those things happen, LetUsPray has succeeded.

⸻

The Product We Hope to Build

We hope to build the most thoughtful, beautiful, peaceful, and biblically faithful prayer experience available.

Not for recognition.

Not for awards.

But because God’s Word deserves our very best.

Every feature should remove distractions.

Every interaction should encourage prayer.

Every decision should honor Genesis.

Everything else is secondary.

⸻

Our Product Vision

When someone opens LetUsPray, we hope they experience one simple invitation:

“Let’s pray.”

One verse.

One prayer.

One conversation.

One heart transformed at a time.

That is the product we are building.
