Chapter 2 — Product Principles

“Every product decision should quietly support the mission.”

Introduction

Features create capability.

Principles create consistency.

Every screen, animation, interaction, notification, and future decision should be measured against these principles.

Technology changes.

These principles should not.

⸻

Principle 1 — Scripture Is Always the Hero

The Bible is never secondary.

Every journey begins with Scripture.

Every prayer responds to Scripture.

The application exists to illuminate God’s Word, never compete with it.

If something distracts from Scripture, it should be redesigned or removed.

⸻

Principle 2 — Prayer Is the Primary Action

Every screen should naturally encourage prayer.

The product should never leave people wondering what to do next.

Read.

Reflect.

Pray.

Continue.

That rhythm defines LetUsPray.

⸻

Principle 3 — Simplicity Creates Peace

Complex interfaces create noise.

Simple interfaces create space.

We remove anything that does not clearly support the mission.

The fewer distractions, the easier it becomes to focus on God.

⸻

Principle 4 — One Purpose Per Screen

Every screen should answer one question.

Home inspires.

Journey guides.

Prayer focuses.

Journal reflects.

Calendar encourages.

Settings configure.

No screen should attempt to accomplish everything.

Clarity always wins.

⸻

Principle 5 — Beauty Should Feel Quiet

Beauty is not decoration.

Beauty is hospitality.

Thoughtful typography.

Comfortable spacing.

Gentle motion.

Natural light.

Soft glass.

Beautiful interfaces should disappear into the experience instead of demanding attention.

⸻

Principle 6 — Reflection Before Completion

We never encourage people to rush.

Prayer is not a checklist.

After each prayer, we intentionally create space for reflection.

Silence is part of the experience.

Personal prayer is part of the experience.

Listening is part of the experience.

⸻

Principle 7 — Celebrate Faithfulness

We celebrate showing up.

Not perfection.

Not streaks.

Not performance.

Every return to prayer is worth celebrating.

Grace always welcomes people back.

⸻

Principle 8 — Build for Everyone

Children.

Students.

Families.

Pastors.

Missionaries.

New believers.

People exploring faith.

People carrying grief.

People celebrating answered prayers.

The product should feel welcoming to everyone while remaining deeply rooted in Scripture.

⸻

Principle 9 — Technology Should Disappear

The greatest compliment someone can give LetUsPray is not,

“That animation was amazing.”

It is,

“I had a wonderful conversation with God.”

Technology succeeds when people forget it exists.

⸻

Principle 10 — Every Detail Matters

The loading animation matters.

The spacing matters.

Accessibility matters.

Offline support matters.

Performance matters.

Privacy matters.

The copy on every button matters.

Nothing is too small.

Everything contributes to the experience.

⸻

Principle 11 — Every Feature Must Answer Why

Before any feature is approved, we ask:

Why does this exist?

How does it help someone pray?

Would Genesis approve?

If we cannot answer those questions clearly, the feature waits.

⸻

Principle 12 — Build Something Worth Returning To

People should not return because they feel obligated.

They should return because spending time with God became one of the most meaningful parts of their day.

If LetUsPray helps create that habit, then the product has fulfilled its purpose.

That is the product we are committed to building.
