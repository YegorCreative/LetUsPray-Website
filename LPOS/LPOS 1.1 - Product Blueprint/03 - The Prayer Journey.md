LPOS 1.1 — Product Blueprint

Chapter 3 — The Prayer Journey

“Every journey begins with a single conversation with God.”

Introduction

People do not open LetUsPray to use an application.

They open it because they desire to pray.

Everything within the product should gently guide them from distraction to reflection, from reading to prayer, and from prayer back into everyday life.

The journey should feel peaceful, intentional, and effortless.

⸻

↓
Welcome
↓

This is the heartbeat of LetUsPray.

Every feature should naturally support this rhythm.

⸻

Home

The Home screen is not a dashboard.

It is an invitation.

When someone opens LetUsPray they should immediately know three things:

• Where they currently are.

• What God is inviting them to today.

• How to begin praying.

Nothing else should compete for attention.

⸻

The Current Journey

If someone has already begun Proverbs…

The app gently invites them to Proverbs 2.

If they are praying through Psalms…

Continue where they left off.

If they are praying the Lord’s Prayer…

Return there.

The app remembers.

People should never wonder where to continue.

⸻

Discover

Sometimes people know exactly what they want.

Sometimes they simply need guidance.

Discover exists to help people find:

• Biblical journeys

• Prayer themes

• Specific books

• Search by verse

• Search by topic

• Featured seasonal journeys

• Recommended next steps

The purpose of Discover is not exploration.

The purpose is helping someone begin praying.

⸻

Prayer Experience

The prayer screen is the center of LetUsPray.

Everything else exists to support this moment.

The rhythm remains constant.

Scripture.

Prayer.

Reflection.

Personal Prayer.

Complete.

This rhythm should remain beautifully consistent throughout the application.

⸻

Personal Reflection

Every completed prayer creates an opportunity.

People may…

Write a journal.

Write their own prayer.

Record an answered prayer.

Save a testimony.

Or simply remain silent.

Reflection is never required.

It is always invited.

⸻

Completing Prayer

Finishing prayer should feel meaningful.

The heartbeat animation reminds people that they spent time with God.

The completion animation is not a reward.

It is a quiet acknowledgment.

A peaceful moment before returning to daily life.

⸻

Returning Home

The journey always returns Home.

Not because prayer is finished.

Because today’s conversation has become tomorrow’s invitation.

Home reflects progress without creating pressure.

It quietly reminds people:

“You spent time with God today.”

⸻

Living Beyond the App

The goal is never to keep people inside LetUsPray.

The goal is helping them carry Scripture into everyday life.

The app ends.

Prayer continues.

Faith continues.

Life continues.

The conversation with God continues.

That is the true destination of every journey.

⸻

Our Design Question

Every future feature should answer one question.

Where does this belong within the Prayer Journey?

If it strengthens the journey, it belongs.

If it interrupts the journey, it should be redesigned.

The Prayer Journey is the blueprint for the entire product.

Everything else is built upon it.
