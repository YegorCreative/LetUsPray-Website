LPOS 1.1 — Product Blueprint

Chapter 4 — The Living Blueprint

“Every feature should have a purpose. Every purpose should serve the mission.”

Introduction

A great product is not a collection of features.

It is a collection of intentional experiences.

Every area within LetUsPray exists for one reason:

To help people encounter God through Scripture and prayer.

This blueprint defines every major part of the product and explains why it belongs.

If a future feature cannot naturally fit within this blueprint, it should be carefully reconsidered before being added.

⸻

🏠 Home

Purpose

Home is the front door of LetUsPray.

It welcomes people back into conversation with God.

Experience

The Home screen should answer three questions immediately:

• Where am I?

• What can I pray today?

• How do I begin?

Home should feel peaceful, focused, and encouraging.

Never overwhelming.

⸻

🙏 Journeys

Purpose

Journeys provide structured ways to pray through Scripture.

Examples include:

• Proverbs

• Psalms

• The Lord’s Prayer

• The Gospels

• Biblical Themes

• Seasonal Journeys

Journeys help people build consistency one chapter and one day at a time.

⸻

📖 Prayer Experience

Purpose

This is the heart of LetUsPray.

Every prayer follows the same rhythm:

Scripture

↓

Prayer

↓

Reflection

↓

Personal Prayer

↓

Completion

The experience remains familiar regardless of the journey being followed.

Consistency creates peace.

⸻

📝 Journal

Purpose

Prayer should leave room for personal conversation.

People may record:

• Personal prayers

• Reflections

• Answered prayers

• Testimonies

• What God is teaching them

Everything remains personal unless intentionally shared.

⸻

📅 Prayer Calendar

Purpose

Celebrate faithfulness.

Not perfection.

The calendar quietly reminds people of the days they spent with God.

Missed days never produce guilt.

Completed days encourage gratitude.

⸻

❤️ Prayer Themes

Purpose

Sometimes people know how they feel.

They simply don’t know where to begin.

Themes help people pray through topics such as:

• Wisdom

• Courage

• Purity

• Forgiveness

• Anxiety

• Hope

• Marriage

• Family

• Leadership

• Missions

• Healing

• Gratitude

Each theme is built entirely upon Scripture.

⸻

🔍 Search

Purpose

Help people find God’s Word quickly.

Search should include:

• Bible verses

• Topics

• Books

• Keywords

• Prayer themes

• Journeys

Finding Scripture should never feel difficult.

⸻

👨‍👩‍👧 Families

Purpose

Encourage families to pray together.

Future experiences may include:

Family prayer journeys.

Prayer reminders.

Praying for one another.

Shared encouragement.

The home remains one of the greatest places where prayer grows.

⸻

👧 Kids

Purpose

Help children discover prayer through Scripture.

Bright illustrations.

Simple language.

Joyful experiences.

Short biblical journeys.

Age-appropriate prayers.

Children deserve thoughtful spiritual experiences designed specifically for them.

⸻

🌍 Missions

Purpose

Expand prayer beyond ourselves.

Pray for:

Missionaries.

Schools.

Cities.

Governments.

Nations.

Churches.

Communities.

Prayer teaches us to care for God’s world.

⸻

💬 Testimonies

Purpose

Share stories of God’s faithfulness.

Not every story belongs inside LetUsPray.

But the right stories encourage faith.

Every testimony should glorify God.

Every testimony should remain authentic.

⸻

🤝 Community (Future)

Purpose

Help believers encourage one another.

Community should always support personal prayer.

Never replace it.

Future possibilities include:

Prayer groups.

Shared journeys.

Church communities.

Encouragement.

Feedback.

Pastor devotionals.

Mission updates.

Everything remains centered on Scripture and prayer.

⸻

👤 Profile

Purpose

One quiet place for the user’s personal journey.

Current journey.

Prayer calendar.

Saved prayers.

Journal.

Personal settings.

Backup options.

Everything that belongs to the individual belongs here.

⸻

⚙️ Settings

Purpose

Settings should remove friction.

Never create it.

Accessibility.

Notifications.

Appearance.

Backup.

Privacy.

Language.

Support.

Feedback.

Simple.

Understandable.

Respectful.

⸻

Living Blueprint

Every future feature should naturally belong somewhere within this blueprint.

If it does…

It strengthens LetUsPray.

If it does not…

It probably belongs somewhere else.

A focused product serves people better than a product that tries to do everything.

This blueprint protects that focus.

One verse.

One prayer.

One conversation with God at a time.
