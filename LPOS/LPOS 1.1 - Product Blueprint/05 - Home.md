LPOS 1.1 — Product Blueprint

Chapter 5 — Home

“Every return should feel like coming home.”

Purpose

Home is the most important screen in LetUsPray.

It is not a dashboard.

It is not a collection of widgets.

It is not a place to display everything the application can do.

Home exists to gently invite people into prayer.

Every decision should support that purpose.

⸻

The Feeling

When someone opens LetUsPray they should immediately feel:

• Peace.

• Simplicity.

• Hope.

• Welcome.

The interface should never feel busy.

People arrive carrying enough from everyday life.

Home should remove noise, not add to it.

⸻

Home Has One Job

Home answers three simple questions.

1. Where am I?

Show the current journey.

If someone is praying Proverbs…

Continue Proverbs.

If they are praying Psalms…

Continue Psalms.

If they are praying through a theme…

Continue the theme.

People should never wonder where to begin.

⸻

2. What can I pray today?

Recommend today’s next step.

Not ten options.

Not endless scrolling.

One clear invitation.

People may always explore further, but Home should never overwhelm.

⸻

3. What has God already done?

Home quietly celebrates faithfulness.

Prayer calendar.

Recent answered prayers.

Current journey progress.

Gentle encouragement.

Home reminds people that God has been walking with them.

⸻

The Visual Hierarchy

The order of information matters.

Nothing should compete with Scripture.

Recommended order:

1. Greeting
2. Continue Journey
3. Today’s Prayer
4. Prayer Calendar
5. Prayer Themes
6. Discover More
7. Recent Reflection
8. Footer Navigation

Each section naturally leads to the next.

⸻

Continue Journey

This is the primary action.

If someone began Proverbs yesterday…

Continue Proverbs today.

The application remembers.

The user simply continues.

The fewer decisions required, the easier it becomes to build consistency.

⸻

Prayer Themes

Sometimes people don’t know what they need.

Home gently offers themes such as:

Wisdom.

Peace.

Courage.

Purity.

Hope.

Gratitude.

Marriage.

Family.

Healing.

Forgiveness.

The goal is not browsing.

The goal is beginning prayer.

⸻

Prayer Calendar

The calendar quietly encourages consistency.

Green represents days spent with God.

Empty days are never failures.

They are simply invitations to return.

The calendar celebrates grace rather than perfection.

⸻

Daily Encouragement

Home may occasionally offer encouragement.

A Scripture.

A testimony.

A reminder.

A simple invitation to pray.

These moments should feel natural.

Never intrusive.

⸻

Search

Search should always remain accessible.

Some people know exactly where they wish to begin.

The fastest path to Scripture should always be available.

⸻

The Home Experience

Someone opens LetUsPray.

Within five seconds they understand:

Where they are.

What they should pray.

How to begin.

Everything else becomes secondary.

Home should quietly whisper one invitation:

“Let’s pray.”

That is the purpose of Home.

Every future redesign should protect it.
