LPOS 1.1 — Product Blueprint

Chapter 7 — Journeys

“Every journey should help someone build a lifelong habit of prayer through Scripture.”

Purpose

Journeys are the primary way people experience LetUsPray.

A journey is more than a reading plan.

It is a guided spiritual experience that combines Scripture, prayer, reflection, and consistency.

Journeys provide structure without removing freedom.

People always know where to continue, but they remain free to explore other prayers whenever they choose.

⸻

Journey Principles

Every journey should:

* Begin with Scripture.
* Encourage prayer.
* Be easy to continue.
* Never create guilt for missed days.
* Celebrate consistency.
* End with a deeper love for God’s Word.

⸻

Journey Types

LetUsPray organizes journeys into several categories.

Scripture Journeys

Pray through complete books or selected portions of Scripture.

Examples:

* Proverbs
* Psalms
* Gospel of John
* Selected teachings of Jesus
* Song of Songs
* Esther
* Ruth
* James
* Philippians

These journeys remain verse-by-verse whenever appropriate.

⸻

Theme Journeys

Designed around a biblical topic rather than one book.

Examples:

* Wisdom
* Courage
* Anxiety & Peace
* Purity
* Marriage
* Family
* Forgiveness
* Gratitude
* Hope
* Leadership
* Healing
* Identity in Christ

Every theme is built entirely from Scripture.

Scripture defines the theme.

The theme never defines Scripture.

⸻

Guided Prayer Journeys

These teach believers how to pray through important biblical prayers.

Examples:

* The Lord’s Prayer
* Prayers of David
* Prayers of Paul
* Prayers of Moses
* Prayers of Hannah
* Prayers of Jesus
* Prayers of the Early Church

The goal is not repetition.

The goal is understanding.

⸻

Seasonal Journeys

Created for specific seasons of life.

Examples:

* Christmas
* Easter
* New Year
* Graduation
* Beginning College
* Missions Month
* Marriage Preparation
* Parenting

Seasonal journeys remain optional and never replace Scripture journeys.

⸻

Journey Progress

Every journey remembers where someone stopped.

Returning should always feel effortless.

One tap should continue exactly where the user left off.

The application remembers.

The user simply continues.

⸻

Recommendations

When someone completes a journey, LetUsPray gently recommends the next step.

Recommendations should feel personal.

Examples:

* Continue Proverbs.
* Begin Psalms.
* Pray through the Lord’s Prayer.
* Explore Courage.
* Return to your current journey.

Recommendations should inspire rather than overwhelm.

⸻

Completion

Completing a journey should feel meaningful.

The application celebrates faithfulness.

It never suggests that spiritual growth has been “finished.”

Every completed journey becomes another invitation to continue walking with God.

⸻

Future Journeys

As LetUsPray grows, new journeys will continue to be added.

Every new journey should answer three questions:

1. Is it rooted in Scripture?
2. Does it encourage prayer?
3. Will it help someone grow spiritually?

If the answer is yes, it belongs.

⸻

Design Requirements

Every journey should provide:

* Clear title
* Short description
* Estimated duration
* Current progress
* Last prayed date
* Continue button
* Save option
* Share option (where appropriate)

Consistency across journeys is more important than visual variety.

Users should always feel familiar with the experience.

⸻

Success

A journey succeeds when it becomes part of someone’s daily rhythm.

The goal is not to finish journeys.

The goal is to help people continue meeting with God through His Word for the rest of their lives.

That is the purpose of every journey within LetUsPray.
