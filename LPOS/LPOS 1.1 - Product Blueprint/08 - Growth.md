LPOS 1.1 — Product Blueprint

Chapter 8 — Growth

“Growth is measured by faithfulness, not perfection.”

Purpose

The purpose of LetUsPray is not simply to help people complete prayers.

The purpose is to help people build a lifelong relationship with God through Scripture and prayer.

Every growth feature should gently encourage consistency without creating guilt, pressure, or comparison.

Growth is personal.

Growth takes time.

Growth belongs to God.

Our responsibility is simply to encourage it.

⸻

Prayer Calendar

The Prayer Calendar is the visual story of someone’s journey.

Every day spent in prayer becomes a quiet reminder of God’s faithfulness.

Completed days are shown with a peaceful green gradient.

Empty days remain invitations, never failures.

The calendar celebrates presence, not perfection.

⸻

Daily Rhythm

LetUsPray encourages one simple habit:

Open Scripture.

Pray.

Reflect.

Live the day.

Tomorrow, return.

The application should never encourage rushing through multiple chapters simply to increase progress.

Depth is always more valuable than speed.

⸻

Prayer Streaks

Streaks should never become the focus.

If included, they exist only to encourage consistency.

Breaking a streak should never produce shame.

Returning after a missed day should always feel like coming home.

Grace always welcomes people back.

⸻

Milestones

Instead of rewarding performance, LetUsPray celebrates faithfulness.

Examples:

• First Prayer

• First Week

• Thirty Days of Prayer

• One Hundred Conversations with God

• First Answered Prayer Recorded

• First Journal Entry

• Completed Proverbs

• Completed Psalms

Milestones celebrate the journey.

They never define spiritual maturity.

⸻

Answered Prayers

One of the most meaningful experiences within LetUsPray should be remembering God’s faithfulness.

People may mark prayers as answered.

Record how God responded.

Attach personal notes.

Revisit moments of gratitude.

Over time, this becomes a personal testimony of God’s work in their lives.

⸻

Personal Prayer Notebook

Every user receives a private prayer notebook.

They may write:

• Personal prayers

• Reflections

• Gratitude

• Prayer requests

• Lessons God is teaching them

• Spiritual goals

The notebook belongs to the individual.

It remains private unless intentionally shared.

⸻

Today’s Focus

Every morning, users may choose three areas they wish to intentionally pray about.

Examples:

• Joy

• Courage

• Peace

• Wisdom

• Patience

• Hope

• Forgiveness

• Purity

The application responds by recommending Scripture and prayer journeys that support those choices.

The goal is not personalization for convenience.

The goal is intentional prayer.

⸻

Gentle Reminders

Reminders should always feel like invitations.

Examples:

“Would you like to spend a few moments with God today?”

“Your Proverbs journey is waiting.”

“The Lord’s Prayer is available whenever you’re ready.”

Notifications should never create guilt.

They should simply remind people that God is always inviting them into conversation.

⸻

Widgets

The Home Screen widget extends prayer beyond the application.

Possible experiences include:

Today’s verse.

Today’s prayer.

Continue Journey.

Prayer reminder.

Answered prayer memory.

The widget should quietly invite people back into Scripture throughout the day.

⸻

Reflection

Growth often happens after prayer.

Not during it.

After completing a prayer, users should always have space to:

Journal.

Write a personal prayer.

Record an answered prayer.

Remain in silence.

Simply close the application.

Reflection is part of spiritual growth.

⸻

Success

Growth succeeds when people begin noticing changes in everyday life.

More peace.

More gratitude.

More wisdom.

More trust.

More conversations with God.

The greatest achievement LetUsPray can help create is not a perfect streak.

It is a heart that continually returns to God.

That is the growth we hope to encourage.
